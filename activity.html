My Music Story

More Than Just A Hobby


Singing is one of my favourite activities because it gives me a way to express emotions that sometimes cannot easily be explained through normal words. Since I was a kid, I used to participate in singing competitions and I was known as "The Singing Star" at my school.


Music helps me relax after a busy day and gives me the confidence to express myself. I enjoy listening to different styles of music and discovering songs from different cultures and languages.


Since I also enjoy learning languages, music gives me another way to experience different cultures. A song can connect people even when they do not speak the same language.


Music will always be the my top escape from reality.
